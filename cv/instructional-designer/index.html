✈︎ Spot the FODSample · made-up content

Spot the FOD

Loose objects on the airfield can cause serious damage. This short module helps you notice them and act.

By the end you can:
1. Say what FOD is.
2. Spot FOD on an apron.
3. Choose the right first action.

⏱ About 3 minutes · 4 steps

What is FOD?

FOD means Foreign Object Debris: any loose object on the airfield that should not be there.

🔩 Small parts
bolts, nuts, pieces of metal
🛍️ Light rubbish
plastic bags, wrappers, cans
🔧 Equipment
tools, pens, ID cards

Even a small object can be sucked into an engine or damage a tyre.

Find 3 pieces of FOD

Tap each object that should not be on the apron. 0 / 3 found

Well spotted: a spanner, a plastic bag and a drink can. All three could be picked up by an engine or by the wind.

What do you do first?

You are walking to an aircraft at Stand 12. You see a metal bolt on the ground near the wheels.
