Why this role

Read my cover letter ＋

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to apply for the Student & Programme Advisor role at Waipapa Taumata Rau | The University of Auckland. For the past 12 years I have worked with adult learners in New Zealand education, and the parts of my work I value most are the ones at the centre of this role: giving students clear advice, keeping their records right, and following up when someone starts to fall behind.

In my current role as an English Tutor at MSL Training / NZIE, I keep attendance, learning records and assessment results in SELMA. Because I see this information every week, I can notice early when a learner's attendance or progress drops, contact them, and refer support needs to the right team. Many of my learners are balancing study with work and family, so I have learned to give advice that is practical and realistic, not just correct.

At Aspiring Language Institute, I combined teaching with enrolment administration from 2024. I prepared offer-of-place letters and invoices for students and their education agents, checked programme details, dates and fees, and kept student information up to date in the school's system. I also prepared enrolment tests and assessments and took part in assessment review and pre-moderation with colleagues. Since 2023 I have also coached adults one-on-one for PTE Academic and IELTS, where I agree a study plan with each learner, track their progress and give honest advice about timing and next steps.

I work comfortably with people from many backgrounds. I have supported refugee, migrant and international learners since 2014, and I speak English and Mandarin. I enjoy working with academic and professional colleagues, and I recently ran a professional development session for fellow teachers on using AI tools in everyday work. I also bring a practical approach to systems: I use Excel and simple digital tools to keep information organised, so the right follow-up happens at the right time.

This role supports students through every stage of their studies, from choosing a programme to completing it. I would welcome the chance to help your students make good decisions along the way and to make sure no one slips through the gaps.

Thank you for considering my application. You can see an interactive version of my CV at bingling103-max.github.io/cv/advisor. I would be glad to talk with you further.

Yours sincerely,
Bingling Liu
