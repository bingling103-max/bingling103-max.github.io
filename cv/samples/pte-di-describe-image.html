Four key words

waxing /ˈwæksɪŋ/

getting biggerThe moon is waxing. = It is getting bigger.

waning /ˈweɪnɪŋ/

getting smallerThe moon is waning. = It is getting smaller.

crescent /ˈkresənt/

a thin curved shapea thin crescent moon

gibbous /ˈɡɪbəs/

more than half litFor 58, just say “more than half”
Memory tip: wax = grow (bigger), wane = shrink (smaller). If you forget a name, say a thin moon / a half moon / a big moon. You still get the marks.

Other useful words

  • lunar cycle the cycle of phases (about 29.5 days)
  • the lit part the bright side we can see
  • orbit the Earth move around the Earth
  • reflect sunlight send back light from the sun
  • get bigger / get smaller
  • we cannot see the moon (new moon)
  • half of the moon is lit (first / last quarter)
  • the whole moon is bright (full moon)

Concentrated vs Scattered · Distribution

Use these words for dots on a map, scatter plots and population maps. Three pictures: concentrated, scattered, even.

Language bank

CONCENTRATED
  • be concentrated in mostly in one area Most people are concentrated in the south.
  • be clustered around grouped around a place The dots are clustered around the city.
  • densely populated many people live here
  • close together near each other (best for 58)
SCATTERED
  • be scattered across found all over an area Small towns are scattered across the country.
  • be spread out not in one place
  • sparsely populated few people live here
  • far apart a long way from each other (best for 58)
EVEN / UNEVEN
  • evenly distributed the same everywhere
  • unevenly distributed more in some places
  • the majority of … are in … most of them are in one place
  • only a few … a small number

How to describe a scatter plot

For 58, say one of two things: dots follow a line going up → The dots are close to a line going up.　dots are everywhere → The dots are far apart, so there is no clear pattern.

Natural Processes

These are all cycle or process pictures. Use Template E for all of them: beginning → middle → end. Even a six-step picture needs only three steps.

Process & Cycle Language

First decide: does it go in one line to the end (a process), or round in a circle back to the start (a cycle)?

PROCESS · LINEAR

a seed grows into a tree, making bread, making paper

CYCLE

moon phases, the water cycle, seasons, life cycles

Language bank

BEGINNING
  • The process begins with …
  • The cycle starts with …
  • First, …
  • At the first stage, …
MIDDLE
  • Next, …
  • Then, …
  • After that, …
  • … turns into / becomes …
END
  • Finally, …
  • At the end, …
  • The cycle repeats. (cycle)
  • It goes back to the start. (cycle)
OVERALL
  • There are four stages in this process.
  • The diagram shows how … changes.
  • It takes about one month / one year.
  • The arrows show the order.

Useful verbs (present simple)

  • grow / get bigger become larger
  • get smaller become less big
  • go up / rise move higher
  • fall / go down move lower
  • turn into / become change into
  • move around go in a circle around
  • block stop the light
  • pull gravity pulls
Two rules: use the present simple all the way through; end a cycle with and the cycle repeats, but not a process.
